# US-01 Authorization Tokenu pri Roaming session

Ako CPO
chcem pri priložení karty vedieť, či je Token Drivera platný,
aby som spustil nabíjanie iba Driverovi, ktorého eMSP za session zaplatí.

## Väzby
- Requirements: R-01, R-02, R-03, R-08
- Business Rules: BR-01, BR-02, BR-03, BR-11
- Business Actor / Role: BA-Member (CPO), ROLE-RoamingDriver
- Business Process: BP-Authorization (EA #109)
- Business Service: BS-AuthorizationService (EA #77)
- Application Services: AS-HubService (EA #120), AS-AuthorizationService (EA #121)
- Components: AC-HubService, AC-IdentityService, AC-MemberBackend
- Scenarios: SC-01-01 … SC-01-04, SC-02-01, SC-02-02, SC-03-01
