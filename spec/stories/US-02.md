# US-02 Prijatie CDR a mesačný Settlement

Ako Finance
chcem, aby do Settlement vstúpili iba overené CDR a každý Member dostal jeden statement s Net balance,
aby sa peniaze medzi Members vyrovnali jednou platbou mesačne.

## Väzby
- Requirements: R-04, R-05, R-06, R-07
- Business Rules: BR-04 … BR-10
- Business Actors: BA-Member (CPO, eMSP), BA-Finance
- Business Processes: BP-BridgeMembers (EA #110), BP-SettlePayment (EA #111)
- Business Services: BS-MemberCommunicationService (EA #78), BS-SettlementService (EA #80)
- Application Service: AS-HubService (EA #120)
- Component: AC-HubService
- Data Objects: DO-CDR, DO-TariffVersion, DO-MemberPaymentClaimRecord, DO-SettlementStatement
- Scenarios: SC-04-01 … SC-04-04, SC-05-01, SC-05-02, SC-06-01, SC-06-02
